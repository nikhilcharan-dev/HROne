## Looked at and left alone

- `load_dotenv()` does not override variables already set in the environment (its default), which is what the environment contract asks for.
- `IST = timezone(timedelta(hours=5, minutes=30))` is correct; India has no daylight saving, so a fixed offset is enough.
- Returning 409 for a duplicate is the right status; only the mechanism (check-then-insert) was wrong.
- `status_code=201` on the two create endpoints, and popping `_id` after `insert_one` (which mutates the dict), are fine.
- String comparison on `date` with `$gte` / `$lte` is correct because `YYYY-MM-DD` sorts chronologically; I kept it and index on it.
- Truncating negative minutes with `int()` in `compute_late_minutes` is harmless: early punch-ins fail the `> 10` threshold either way.
